Election Livestreams

Page Content

​​​​​Section 127.1232(b) of the Texas Election Code requires the general custodian of election records in a county with a population over 100,000 to establish a video recording system that captures all areas containing voted ballots from the time that the ballots are delivered to the central counting station (CCS), early voting ballot board (EVBB), or signature verification committee (SVC) until the local canvass of election results. The video recording must also be made available as a livestream during that timeframe.
