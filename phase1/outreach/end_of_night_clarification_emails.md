## 1. Collin County

To: kbreaux@collincountytx.gov
Subject: Question about election night precinct results for November 3, 2026

Dear Kaleb Breaux and the Collin County Elections staff,

My name is David Logan, and I am the Data Director for the Jon Rosenthal for Railroad Commission campaign. I am compiling how Texas counties publish results on election night and would appreciate a short clarification for Collin County.

From your public results pages, we found an unofficial precinct-by-precinct report dated election night for the November 2024 general election. For the November 3, 2026 general election:

1. Will precinct-by-precinct results be posted online on election night?
2. Will they be posted once with the final unofficial count, or updated more than once during the night?
3. About what time does the first precinct-level report usually go up, and where on your site will it be?

A short reply is plenty. Thank you for your time.

Data Director, Jon Rosenthal for Railroad Commission
