Elections

The function of the Smith County Elections Administration is to register voters and maintain accurate voter registration rolls for the 66 Smith County precincts. The office also conducts all countywide and special elections, as well as elections conducted under interlocal agreements.

The goal of the Elections Administration is to provide and protect the voting rights of the citizens of Smith County and assure them that the vote they cast will be effectively and accurately tabulated.

Voting for People with Disabilities

"In accordance with the requirements of Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 ("ADA"), Smith County will not discriminate against qualified individuals with disabilities on the basis of disability in its services, programs, or activities. Anyone who requires an auxiliary aid or service for effective communication, or a modification of policies or procedures to participate in a program, service, or activity of Smith County, should email the ADA Coordinator, Esmeralda Corona, mail her at 200 E Ferguson Street, Suite 202, Tyler, TX 75702, call 903-590-4645, or submit an ADA Accommodation Request."

Making voting accessible to all eligible voters is critical. If you ever see or experience anything that you feel is inaccessible, please email the Smith County Elections Administration, call 903-590-4777 or email the ADA Coordinator, Esmeralda Corona, mail her at 200 E Ferguson Street, Suite 202, Tyler, TX 75702, call 903-590-4645, or submit an ADA Accommodation Request.

Curbside Voting

Curbside voting is available at all polling places. Look for the Curbside Voter Parking sign located at each polling place, and give us a call; or if someone has accompanied you, they can go into the polling place to request assistance. We will bring the ballot to you in the vehicle. Curbside voting is reserved for voters who are physically unable to enter the polling place without personal assistance or the likelihood of injuring the voter's health (Texas Election Code Sec. 65.009).

Polling Place Accessibility

All polling locations in Smith County are accessible to voters with disabilities, as is all the voting equipment. The locations are ground-floor accessible with at least 32-inch wide doorways and curb cuts for parking and entering the building. If locations have stairways, there are ramps or other entrances that can be used. The voting machines have multiple functions that make them accessible to all voters: there is an audio version of the ballot and a braille keypad, the text can be magnified or the screen contrast changed to high resolution black and white, and there is a sip and puff connection.

Assistants

Persons who are unable to read or write or who need physical help with the ballot are able to bring an assistant of their choice to help with the voting process. Only the voter's union representative and the employer are ineligible to assist. A voter's assistant must sign an oath before they can actively begin to assist the voter with their ballot. Election workers are also available upon request to assist.

Ballot by Mail

Voters with disabilities are also allowed to request a ballot by mail. They can request a ballot for a single election or for all ballots they are eligible for in that calendar year. They can request an application from our office, or they can print the application (English (PDF), Spanish (PDF)) and mail it to our office. We have to receive an application from the voter before we can mail the ballot.

Get more information on services available in Texas for voters with disabilities.

FAQs

How do I verify that I am registered?

To verify the fact that you are registered, you can obtain information from the Secretary of State.

You may search by one of three methods:

  • Your Voter Unique Identifier (VUID), which appears on your voter registration certificate
  • Your Texas driver's license number, if you provided it when you applied for voter registration
  • Your first and last name

What if I've changed my name?

You have three different methods of updating your new name:

  • Online
    • Contact the Texas Department of Public Safety
  • In Person
    • Smith County Courthouse Annex
      302 E Ferguson
      Tyler, Texas 75702

      For questions, call or fax:
      Phone: 903-590-4777
      Fax: 903-590-4778
  • By Mail
    • Request an application be mailed to you
    • Mail completed applications to:
      Smith County Elections Administration
      302 E Ferguson
      Tyler, Texas 75702

What if I've moved recently?

You must be registered at your new address thirty days prior to an election. You have three different methods of updating your new address:

  • Online Address Changes
    • If you have moved within the county, you may change your name or address online. Take advantage of this service provided by the Secretary of State.
  • In Person
    • Address or name changes may be made in our office located at:
      Smith County Elections Administration
      302 E Ferguson
      Tyler, Texas 75702
  • By Mail
    • Request an application be mailed to you.
    • Sign your name and fill out the spaces provided with your new address. Mail the certificate to the following address:
      Smith County Elections Administration
      302 E Ferguson
      Tyler, Texas 75702

What if my Drivers License is not current?

You can contact the DPS in one of two ways:

  • Online
  • In person at the following address:
    4700 University Blvd.
    Tyler, Texas 75713
    Phone: 903-939-6014

Can I vote by mail?

To vote by mail, you must request a ballot for each election. To be eligible to vote by mail in Texas, you must have one of these conditions:

  • Be 65 years or older
  • Be disabled
  • Be out of the county on election day and during the period for early voting by personal appearance
  • Be confined in jail, but otherwise eligible

View Voting by Mail for specific information.

Request an application be mailed to you. Completed registration applications should then be mailed to:
