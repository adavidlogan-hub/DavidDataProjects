                                   Tyler County Polling Place Guidelines 
 
In Order to Vote, Citizens must:

*   Be on the Official List of Registered Voters
*   We CAN accept your ID ONLY if it has been EXPIRED no more than 4 years.
*   Citizens over the age of 70, may use an EXPIRED ID, no matter the length, in order to vote.
*   We CAN accept your Ballot by Mail in our office up to 7 pm on Election Night.
