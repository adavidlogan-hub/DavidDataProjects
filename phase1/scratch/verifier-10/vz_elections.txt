The main Early Voting location has moved to the
new
Courthouse Annex, 1900 N. Trade Days Blvd., Canton (the old Paul Michael building).  The new location is next to the Taco Shack, in front of Mill Creek RV Park and across the street from Taco Bell.
Early Voting will not be conducted at the Van Zandt County Library.
Voting will take place October 19th – October 30th.  For voting times, please see the information below in the November 3rd 2026 Election link.
[[http://www.votetexas.gov/]] Votetexas.gov - This site will allow individuals to check voter registration status, register to vote (must print out and mail to the voter registrar's office), check for polling locations, etc.
